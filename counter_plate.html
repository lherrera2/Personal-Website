PLATE SHELF COUNTER SYSTEM

Designed a production-ready serving counter with integrated plate storage, translating architectural layouts into a manufacturable system for commercial installation.

Impact

• Delivered production-ready counter system for real-world installation
• Improved service efficiency through integrated plate storage
• Translated architectural layouts into manufacturable assemblies

Constraints

• Fixed architectural layout and spatial constraints
• Required compatibility with foodservice equipment
• Needed to be manufacturable using standard sheet metal processes

Approach

1. Interpreted architectural layouts and requirements
2. Developed structural frame using weldments
3. Integrated storage and service components into assembly
4. Prepared manufacturing-ready drawings and models

PROJECT CONTEXT

Developed for a high-end hospitality environment requiring precise fit, durability, and clean integration.

The system needed to operate efficiently within a constrained footprint while supporting service workflow and integrated storage.

DESIGN INPUT

Began with AutoCAD layouts defining spatial constraints, equipment placement, and workflow requirements.

These inputs established the foundation for a fully detailed and manufacturable 3D system.

ENGINEERING DESIGN

Developed the structural frame in SolidWorks using weldments to balance strength, manufacturability, and assembly efficiency.

  • Structural support for equipment and shelving
  • Integrated plate storage within the frame
  • Sheet metal–friendly geometry for fabrication

FINAL ASSEMBLY

Integrated structure, storage, and service surfaces into a cohesive assembly ready for fabrication and installation.

RESULT

Delivered a production-ready system that translated directly from CAD to fabrication and installation.

Enabled reliable operation in a high-traffic commercial environment while maintaining design intent.
