STEWART PLATFORM KINEMATICS SIMULATOR

Real-time inverse and forward kinematics with singularity detection, workspace constraints, and dynamic motion validation for a 6-DOF parallel manipulator.

ENGINEERING SUMMARY

Impact

• Developed a full 6-DOF Stewart platform simulator for motion control and workspace validation
• Built inverse kinematics for direct platform positioning and forward kinematics for pose reconstruction
• Added singularity detection to identify unstable platform positions before failure could occur
• Applied actuator stroke limits and workspace constraints to ensure safe, realistic motion

Challenges

• Solving forward kinematics with multiple possible platform positions
• Preventing unstable motion near singular configurations
• Keeping all movement within safe and physically reachable limits

Engineering Value

Stewart platforms are used in aerospace docking systems, flight simulators, robotic positioning systems, and precision manufacturing. This project demonstrates practical understanding of motion control, system constraints, and reliable operation in complex mechanical systems.

INVERSE KINEMATICS

Inverse kinematics allows direct control of platform position and orientation by calculating the required actuator lengths for each desired pose. This ensures stable motion while respecting stroke limits and avoiding invalid configurations.

FORWARD KINEMATICS

Forward kinematics reconstructs platform position from actuator lengths using an iterative nonlinear solver. This validates real platform behavior and confirms the system remains within the valid workspace.

DIAMOND TRAJECTORY

Structured motion profile used to validate controlled traversal of workspace extremes while maintaining stable actuator coordination.

COINING MOTION

Continuous coordinated tilt and rotation demonstrating dynamic multi-axis control and realistic actuation behavior across constrained motion paths.

SINGULARITY ANALYSIS

Certain platform positions create singularities where small actuator movements can cause large, unstable platform motion. These unsafe conditions were detected using Jacobian condition number analysis and automatically rejected before they could affect system stability.

Condition Number: 2.3
Normal operating region with predictable motion, reliable solver convergence, and safe actuator response.

Singular Configuration

Unstable pose where very small actuator changes create large platform movement and unreliable system behavior.

FINAL SYSTEM

Complete integrated simulation of inverse kinematics, forward kinematics, motion control, and constraint validation.

KEY INSIGHT

Forward kinematics in Stewart platforms is highly sensitive to starting conditions and physical constraints. Reliable motion control depends on solver stability, workspace validation, and avoiding singular positions that can create unsafe platform behavior.
